Transparency

Corrections Policy

We fix errors quickly, openly, and with a clear notice.

Our commitment

Accuracy is the foundation of this newsroom. When we get something wrong — a name, date, venue, release detail, or quote — we correct it as soon as we verify the right information, and we do it openly so readers can see what changed.

How corrections work

  • Minor fixes (typos, formatting, broken links): fixed silently; the article's modification date updates automatically.
  • Material corrections (wrong facts, dates, names): fixed in the article body plus a dated Correction notice appended to the article stating what was wrong and what it now reads.
  • Retractions: if an article's core claim proves false, it is withdrawn and replaced with a retraction notice explaining why.

Report an error

Spotted a mistake? Email contact@younghadene.ca with the article URL and what's wrong. We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and publish corrections within 5 business days of verification.
