About the album

Sacrifices & Success is a 9-track album from Young Hadene, released on January 10, 2025 through Str8hitsRecords.1 It runs about 14 minutes and is marked explicit on Apple Music.1

Born in Haiti, with part of his childhood in Florida, Young Hadene is a Toronto-based artist who releases everything independently. Read his Haitian-Toronto story or visit the about page for more background. Sacrifices & Success came out on January 10, 2025.

The sound

Young Hadene's lane is Toronto drill and dark trap. For background, see what Toronto drill music is and dark trap vs. drill. Most tracks here come in under two minutes. "Keep In Touch" is the longest, at a little over three.1
