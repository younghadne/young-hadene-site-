Young Hadene – "Broken"

About the track

"Broken" is a song by Young Hadene, a rapper who was born in Haiti, spent part of his childhood in Florida, and now makes music in Toronto. He releases his music independently through Str8hitsRecords.

The official music video went up on the Young Hadene YouTube channel on September 22, 2026. The channel describes the video as "a raw look at late-night urban isolation."

Want the bigger picture? Read Young Hadene's story on the about page.

The sound

"Broken" sits where Young Hadene's two main lanes meet: Toronto drill and dark trap. The YouTube channel tags it as dark trap, Toronto drill, Canadian hip hop and sad trap, and calls its mood mellow and cold. The track runs about two minutes. The video was released by Young Hadene on his own YouTube channel, alongside the rest of his visuals.

In the video, Young Hadene moves through parking lots and city streets at night. The look is stark and gritty, which suits the track's cold atmosphere. If you like the darker, moodier side of drill, start here. The channel also points fans to younghadene.ca for more music and updates, so this page is the official home for the track.

Young Hadene's catalogue includes the album Sacrifices & Success (2025), plus the 11-track They Took Me Down, released October 2, 2026. You'll find all of them on the music page. For news and new releases, head back to the home page.
