Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Young Hadene is an independent recording artist based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. This site (younghadene.ca) is the official website. Contact: contact@younghadene.ca.

What we collect

Basic anonymous analytics (pages visited, referrer, device type) to understand traffic. If you email us or subscribe to updates, we receive the name and email address you provide — used only to respond or send the updates you requested.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data, run third-party ad trackers, or share your information with marketers.

Embedded content

Pages embed players from Spotify and YouTube. Those services may set their own cookies and collect data per their own privacy policies when you play content.

Email updates

Marketing emails go only to addresses with recorded, eligible consent — confirmed signups or another documented lawful basis. Imported addresses without consent are never mailed until the recipient confirms. We store your address, consent record (source, purpose, date, privacy-notice version), and subscription status. Every marketing email identifies the sender, includes our contact information, and carries a working one-click unsubscribe link; unsubscribing needs no account or login and takes effect immediately. Unsubscribed addresses, hard bounces, and complaints are kept on a permanent suppression list — storing only what is needed to prevent further unwanted mail — and are never mailed again or reactivated by re-import. Manage your subscription anytime at younghadene.ca/unsubscribe.

Your rights

Email contact@younghadene.ca to request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us.
