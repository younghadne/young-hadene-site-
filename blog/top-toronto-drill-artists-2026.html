The Toronto Drill Sound

Toronto drill artists took the sliding 808s and ominous piano loops of Chicago drill and filtered them through the 6ix: more melody, more atmosphere, more introspection. The result is darker and more cinematic than its American cousin — and in 2026 it's the default language of Toronto street rap.

The Pioneers & Heavy Hitters

Presa — The Blueprint

Presa proved Toronto drill could travel, linking with UK heavyweights and setting the template for cross-Atlantic drill records.

Yung Tory & Robin Banks — The Workhorses

Relentless output and street credibility kept Toronto drill visible through every era shift. Their catalogs are the genre's backbone in the city.

Smiley — The Breakout

Smiley's off-kilter flow took 6ix drill to OVO cosigns and beyond, proving the underground sound could go mainstream without softening.
