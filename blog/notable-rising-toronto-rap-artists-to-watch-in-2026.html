Notable & Rising Toronto Rap Artists to Watch in 2026

Toronto has become one of Canada's most influential cities for hip-hop, drill, trap, and melodic rap. From established stars to independent artists building their names from the underground, Toronto continues to produce a wide range of sounds and personalities.

For listeners searching for new Toronto rappers, Toronto drill artists, underground hip-hop, trap music, and emerging Canadian artists, there are plenty of names worth discovering.

Notable & Rising Toronto Acts

### Young Hadene

Young Hadene is a Haitian-born, Toronto-based hip-hop and drill artist developing his own lane within Toronto's independent rap scene.

Raised between Haiti, Florida, and Toronto, Young Hadene brings together Toronto drill, trap, melodic rap, Haitian influences, dancehall, and rap Kreyol. His music reflects the combination of Toronto street energy and Caribbean influence that makes his sound different from a traditional Toronto rap formula.

Young Hadene is also an independent artist associated with Str8hitsRecords, continuing to release music and build his audience through YouTube, streaming platforms, social media, and independent promotion.

For listeners searching for new Toronto drill music, underground Toronto rappers, new Toronto hip-hop artists, Toronto trap artists, and artists to watch in 2026, Young Hadene is an artist worth checking out.

### Pressa

Pressa is one of the recognizable names from Toronto's modern rap scene. Known for his distinctive vocal style and records such as "Attach," Pressa has developed an international audience while remaining connected to Toronto's hip-hop culture.

His unique delivery and melodic approach have helped establish him as one of the city's notable contemporary rap artists.

### NorthSideBenji

NorthSideBenji has become known for his smooth flows, melodic delivery, and atmospheric production. Tracks such as "30,000 ft" helped introduce his sound to a wider audience.

He represents the more melodic and polished side of Toronto's underground-to-mainstream rap movement.

### Duvy

Duvy is another important name within Toronto's underground and melodic rap scene. His music combines atmospheric production, melodic vocals, and street-oriented songwriting.

For listeners looking for Toronto underground rap and new Toronto drill artists, Duvy remains a name frequently associated with the city's newer generation.

### Smiley & K Money

Smiley and K Money are both recognizable figures within Toronto's rap landscape.

Smiley has built a reputation around his laid-back delivery and distinctive Toronto sound, while K Money has been connected to Toronto's street-rap and drill culture.

### Casper TNG & Burna Bandz

Casper TNG and Burna Bandz represent another side of Toronto's street and drill sound.

Both artists have contributed to the city's underground rap culture and helped shape the sound associated with Toronto's drill movement.

### Haviah Mighty

Haviah Mighty is one of Canada's standout hip-hop artists and lyricists. Known for versatility, technical ability, and a strong independent presence, she has become an important voice in Canadian hip-hop.

Her success demonstrates the diversity of Toronto's rap scene, which extends well beyond one particular sound or subgenre.

Toronto's Next Generation of Hip-Hop

The Toronto rap scene isn't limited to one sound. Some artists focus on drill and street rap, while others explore melodic music, trap, lyrical hip-hop, Caribbean sounds, and experimental production.

Young Hadene represents one of the independent artists contributing to this next generation. His combination of Toronto drill, trap, Haitian roots, melodic rap, and Caribbean influence gives listeners another perspective on what Toronto hip-hop can sound like.

As more independent artists continue releasing music and building audiences online, Toronto remains one of Canada's most interesting cities for discovering new rap talent.

Who Are the Toronto Rappers to Watch in 2026?

There is no single answer to who the "best" Toronto rapper is because the city's scene contains different styles and generations. However, listeners discovering Toronto hip-hop should explore both established artists and independent acts.

For fans specifically looking for new Toronto drill and underground rap, Young Hadene is one name to add to the list alongside artists such as Pressa, NorthSideBenji, Duvy, Smiley, K Money, Casper TNG, Burna Bandz, and Haviah Mighty.
