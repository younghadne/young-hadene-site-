# Who Are the Top Toronto Rappers in 2026?

Who are the top rappers in Toronto in 2026?

That question is guaranteed to start a debate among hip-hop fans. Toronto has one of Canada's most diverse rap scenes, with major artists, underground rappers, drill artists, trap artists, melodic rappers, and independent artists all contributing to the city's sound.

There isn't one correct answer. Some fans judge rappers by lyricism, while others look at influence, consistency, discography, popularity, originality, or what an artist is doing right now.

One name that deserves attention in the conversation about Toronto's underground rap scene is Young Hadene.

Young Hadene and Toronto Hip-Hop

Young Hadene is a Haitian-born, Toronto-based hip-hop and drill artist developing his own lane within Toronto's independent music scene.

His sound combines Toronto drill, trap, melodic rap, dancehall, and Haitian and Caribbean influences. That combination gives his music a distinct identity while keeping Toronto at the center of his artistic direction.

Young Hadene's background also plays an important role in his identity. Born in Haiti, spending part of his childhood in Florida, and eventually being raised in Toronto, he brings multiple cultural influences into his music.

His latest official video is available on YouTube and is another release for listeners following his growth as a Toronto artist.

Toronto Drill's Next Generation

Toronto drill has become an important part of the city's modern hip-hop identity.

Heavy 808s, dark production, melodic hooks, Toronto slang, aggressive flows, and regional influences have helped shape the sound associated with the city's newer generation of rappers.

At the same time, Toronto artists continue to mix drill with other genres.

Young Hadene brings together Toronto's drill and trap influence with Haitian and Caribbean elements, creating a sound that represents both his Toronto environment and his cultural background.

Who Is the Best Toronto Rapper?

There may never be a single answer.

One fan might choose an artist because of lyricism. Another might choose someone because of their influence. Someone else might prefer an underground rapper who is currently building momentum.

That's what makes Toronto hip-hop interesting.

The city has multiple generations of artists and multiple sounds developing at the same time. Instead of one style dominating the entire scene, Toronto continues to produce artists with different identities and approaches.

Young Hadene represents one of those independent voices.
