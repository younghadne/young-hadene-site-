# Toronto Underground Rap: Best Underground Rappers and Artists to Know in 2026

Toronto's underground rap scene is one of the most active and recognizable underground hip-hop movements in Canada. The sound is built around gritty street-level lyricism, atmospheric melodies, heavy 808 production, dark trap beats, Toronto drill, melodic rap, and Caribbean influences.

From Regent Park, Rexdale, Jane & Finch, Scarborough, North York, Etobicoke, and communities throughout the Greater Toronto Area, Toronto underground rappers continue to develop new sounds and influence the city's hip-hop culture.

In 2026, listeners searching for Toronto underground rappers, Toronto drill artists, new Toronto rappers, upcoming Toronto artists, Toronto hip-hop artists, and Toronto artists to watch have more artists than ever to discover.

## Top Toronto Underground Artists

Top5 is one of the recognizable names connected to Toronto's modern underground and street-rap scene. His music and online presence helped demonstrate the power of Toronto's underground movement and its ability to generate attention through digital platforms.

Casper TNG is a veteran of Toronto underground rap and street music. Known for his distinctive delivery and influential catalog, Casper TNG remains an important name for anyone exploring Toronto drill, Toronto street rap, and underground hip-hop.

Houdini remains one of the most influential names from Toronto's modern underground rap era. His melodic delivery and emotional style helped define an important period of Toronto street rap, and his music continues to reach new listeners.

Smoke Dawg was a foundational figure in Toronto's underground movement. His connection to Regent Park, raw delivery, collaborations, and international recognition helped put Toronto's street-rap sound in front of a much larger audience.

NorthSideBenji is known for his melodic flow, atmospheric production, and technical delivery. His sound represents another side of Toronto underground rap and shows how melodic music can exist alongside the city's darker street-rap sound.

100 Band Plan represents another developing side of Toronto's underground scene, combining Toronto's dark production style with Caribbean-influenced rhythms.
