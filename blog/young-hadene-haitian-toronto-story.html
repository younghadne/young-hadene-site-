Young Hadene: Haiti to Toronto Drill Story

Young Hadene is a drill and dark trap artist who was born in Haiti, spent part of his childhood in Florida, and now makes music in Toronto. He releases independently through Str8hitsRecords. This page covers where he comes from, what his music sounds like, what he has released so far, and where to start listening.

If you searched "Yung Hadene" or "Young Haden," you're in the right place. The name is Young Hadene.

Haiti, Florida, Toronto

Three places shaped Young Hadene's story. He was born in Haiti. Part of his childhood was spent in Florida. Today he's based in Toronto, which is where he records and releases his music.

Moving between countries as a kid means learning new schools, new accents and new rules more than once. A lot of artists who grew up that way end up writing about the same things: loyalty to the people who stayed with you, pressure to make the move worth it, and the distance between where you started and where you are now. Those themes run all through drill and dark trap, and they fit an artist whose life has already crossed a few borders.

Toronto matters to the story too. The city has one of the most recognizable rap scenes in the world, and drill has been part of it for years. Making drill in Toronto means working in a place where fans already know the sound well and are quick to tell when something is borrowed instead of lived. For more on that background, see our guide to what Toronto drill music is.

The sound: drill rhythm and dark trap atmosphere

Young Hadene's music sits between two styles.

Drill brings the rhythm. Sliding 808 basslines, skittering hi-hats and off-beat snares give the tracks their urgency. Verses tend to be direct and punchy.

Dark trap brings the mood. Minor-key melodies, heavy low end, space in the mix and a cinematic, late-night feel make the songs sound bigger than a standard drill record.

On top of that, he leans on melodic hooks. Instead of staying in one gear for a whole song, tracks switch between aggressive verses and hooks you can sing back. That contrast is the core of the sound. If you want the technical side of how these two genres differ, read Dark Trap vs Drill: Sound, Songwriting & 808s.

Independent through Str8hitsRecords

Young Hadene releases his music independently through Str8hitsRecords. Being independent means the creative decisions stay with the artist: when to drop, what the artwork looks like, which songs make an album, and how the music gets to fans.

It also means the work of getting heard falls on the artist and the small team around him rather than a major label's marketing department. Every stream, share and playlist add counts. If you're an artist trying to do the same thing, our Toronto rap promotion playbook covers the practical side.

Discography: albums

Two of his albums are a good place to start.

They Took Me Down (2026)

They Took Me Down is an 11-track album released on October 2, 2026. It's his most recent project and the best place to start if you're new. The album was covered by Exposed Vocals, which you can find linked on the press page.

Sacrifices & Success (2025)

Sacrifices & Success came out January 10, 2025, with nine tracks. The title sums up a theme that comes up again and again in his writing: what it costs to chase something, and what you get if you keep going.

Singles

Alongside the albums, Young Hadene has put out several singles:

  • "No Sleep"
  • "6ix Side": "the 6ix" is the common nickname for Toronto, so the title points straight at the city.
  • "Pressure"
  • "After Dark"

The singles are a quick way to hear the range of the sound before committing to a full album. All of them are linked from the music page.

Where to listen

Young Hadene's music is on the major streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and SoundCloud. The easiest way to find every release in one place is the music page on this site, which links out to each platform.

A simple listening order for new fans:

  1. Start with They Took Me Down, the newest album, from top to bottom.
  2. Play the singles "No Sleep," "6ix Side," "Pressure" and "After Dark."
  3. Go back to Sacrifices & Success (2025) to hear the earlier work.
