Young Hadene "No Sleep": What We Know

People search for the meaning of "No Sleep" a lot. To be straight with you: Young Hadene hasn't published official lyrics or an explanation of the song that we can point to. So rather than guess at what every line means, this post sticks to what's confirmed and leaves the interpretation to you.

The facts

  • Artist: Young Hadene, a Haitian-born, Toronto-based drill and dark trap artist.
  • Release: "No Sleep" is one of his singles, alongside "6ix Side", "Pressure" and "After Dark".
  • Label: released independently through Str8hitsRecords.

The sound

Like the rest of his catalogue, it sits in drill and dark trap territory. If you want to know what to listen for in that style, such as the 808s, the drums and the space left for the vocals, read Dark Trap vs Drill and Toronto Drill Music: The Complete Guide.

Listening for meaning yourself

The title is the only thing we can say for sure. If you want to dig into the lyrics, listen closely and read them in context instead of trusting a third-party lyric site's guess. How to Write Drill Lyrics & Hooks breaks down how drill verses are usually built, which helps when you're listening closely.

If Young Hadene shares more about the song, we'll update this page.

Where to listen
