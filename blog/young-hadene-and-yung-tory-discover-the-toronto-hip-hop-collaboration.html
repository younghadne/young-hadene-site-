# Young Hadene and Young Torys: Discover the Toronto Hip-Hop Collaboration

Young Hadene continues to build his presence in Canadian hip-hop with music that connects Toronto drill, trap, and modern rap influences. For listeners searching for new Toronto rappers, Canadian drill music, and emerging hip-hop artists, Young Hadene is an artist worth discovering.

Born in Haiti and raised between Florida and Toronto, Young Hadene brings a multicultural background to his musical identity. His connection to Toronto's rap scene and his Haitian roots help shape his artistic direction. Through independent music releases and online promotion, he continues to introduce his sound to listeners looking for new Canadian rap music.
