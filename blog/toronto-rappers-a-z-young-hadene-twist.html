Why the twist?

Most A-Z lists stop at history. Toronto didn't stop. From Maestro and Kardinal to Drake, the Weeknd-era mood, drill pressure and melodic runs — the city keeps reinventing. This version plants Hadene in that lineage: independent, Toronto-made, dark trap. If you searched “best Toronto rappers / rappers from Toronto / Toronto rappers A-Z” — you found home.
