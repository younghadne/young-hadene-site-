Young Hadene and the Toronto Underground

Toronto's underground is huge. Hundreds of independent artists across the city put out drill, trap and everything in between, often without a label or a big playlist behind them. This post doesn't rank anyone against anyone. Comparisons like that are mostly opinion, and taste varies. Instead, it looks at what the underground generally looks like and where Young Hadene fits in it.

What "underground" usually means in Toronto

In practice it tends to mean:

  • Independent releases. Artists put music out themselves or through small labels.
  • Building from the ground up. Fans find music through local shows, YouTube, word of mouth and smaller playlists.
  • Range of styles. Drill, dark trap, melodic rap and more all sit side by side.

For more on the city's sound and history, see Toronto Rap Scene 2026: Sound, Culture, History, and for names to follow, Upcoming Toronto Rappers to Watch.

Where Young Hadene fits

Young Hadene fits that independent model. He releases through Str8hitsRecords and works in drill and dark trap. What sets his path apart is his background: born in Haiti, part of his childhood in Florida, and now based in Toronto. Read more in his Haitian-Toronto story.

He also has a full catalogue behind him rather than only loose singles:

  • They Took Me Down: 11 tracks, October 2, 2026, on Apple Music.
  • Sacrifices & Success: 9 tracks, January 10, 2025.
  • Singles: "No Sleep", "6ix Side", "Pressure" and "After Dark".
  • The "Broken" music video, released September 22, 2026.

Exposed Vocals has also interviewed him.

Judge for yourself

The fairest comparison is your own. Play They Took Me Down next to the other Toronto artists you already listen to and see how it sits. Latest Toronto Drill Songs & Releases has tips for finding more new music, and everything from Young Hadene is on the music page.
