Toronto continues to be one of Canada's most important cities for hip-hop. **Drake** remains one of the defining names in Toronto music and one of the artists most responsible for putting Toronto hip-hop on the global map.

His combination of rap, R&B, melody, Toronto references, and international collaborations helped establish Toronto as a major global music city.

When people search for **best Toronto rappers, top Toronto rappers, Toronto hip-hop artists, or famous rappers from Toronto**, Drake is inevitably part of the conversation.

Although primarily associated with R&B and alternative pop, **The Weeknd** is an essential part of Toronto's broader hip-hop and urban music identity.

His dark atmospheric production and Toronto-centered artistic identity helped create an international image of the city as a place where darker, emotional, genre-blending music could thrive.

His influence continues to be felt throughout Toronto's music ecosystem.

## 3. K'naan

K'naan represents another important part of Canada's multicultural hip-hop history.
