Top Toronto Rappers to Know: 15 Artists, From Maestro to Now

Toronto has been making rap records since the 1980s, long before the rest of the world started calling it the 6ix. This page is a starting point for anyone who wants to hear that history in order: the pioneers who proved a Canadian rapper could get signed and go platinum, the artists who carried the city into the streaming era, and the newer names working in drill, trap and darker, moodier rap.

This site is run by Young Hadene's team. Every artist on this list gets the same format and the same kinds of facts. Young Hadene is one of the 15 artists below. We put him on the list because this is his site, not because of any chart position, and we've held his entry to the same rules as everyone else's.

How this list works

This is not a ranking, and there is no number one. "Best" depends on what you value, whether that's sales, lyricism, influence or what you play on the way home, and we don't have a fair way to measure that across 40 years of music. Instead, the artists are grouped by era, and every entry covers the same kinds of facts:

  • From / based: where the artist was born or is based, as the source states it.
  • Active since: the first year the source gives for their career or first release.
  • Releases to know: two or three records the source names, with years.
  • Why they're here: one documented fact about their place in Toronto rap.
  • Start with: one release to play first.

Every fact links to a public source in the Sources section at the bottom. If we couldn't source something, we left it out. We don't list streams, follower counts or "most-played" claims for anyone, because those numbers change daily and we can't check them fairly for all 15 artists.

A note on geography: most artists here were born or raised in the City of Toronto. Two are from elsewhere in the region or arrived later, and we say so in their entries.

The pioneers (1980s–1990s)

Maestro Fresh Wes

  • From / based: Toronto.1
  • Active since: 1987.1
  • Releases to know: Symphony in Effect (1989), including the single "Let Your Backbone Slide".1
  • Why they're here: Symphony in Effect was the first platinum-certified album by a Black Canadian artist, and "Let Your Backbone Slide" was the first rap song inducted into the Canadian Songwriters Hall of Fame.1
  • Start with: "Let Your Backbone Slide".

Michie Mee

  • From / based: Born in Kingston, Jamaica; raised in Toronto's Jane Street area.2
  • Active since: 1985.2
  • Releases to know: "Elements of Style", her first single with DJ L.A. Luv; the duo's tracks on the 1987 compilation Break'n Out.2
  • Why they're here: In 1988 she became the first Canadian MC signed to a major American label (First Priority/Atlantic).2
  • Start with: "Elements of Style".

Kardinal Offishall

  • From / based: Toronto.3
  • Active since: 1994, in the city's mid-1990s underground scene.3
  • Releases to know: Eye & I (1997), Quest for Fire: Firestarter, Vol. 1 (2001), Not 4 Sale (2008).3
  • Why they're here: His 2001 single "BaKardi Slang" popularized "T-dot" as a nickname for Toronto, and his style draws heavily on reggae and dancehall.3
  • Start with: "BaKardi Slang".

k-os

  • From / based: Toronto.4
  • Active since: 1995, with the single "Musical Essence".4
  • Releases to know: Exit (2002), Joyful Rebellion (2004), Atlantis: Hymns for Disco (2006).4
  • Why they're here: He mixes rap with funk, rock and reggae, often plays with a live band, and wrote and produced most of his albums himself. Joyful Rebellion went platinum in Canada.4
  • Start with: Joyful Rebellion.

The streaming-era wave (2000s–2010s)

Drake

  • From / based: Toronto.5
  • Active since: 2001 (acting on Degrassi: The Next Generation); his first mixtape came in 2006.5
  • Releases to know: So Far Gone (2009), Take Care (2011), Views (2016).5
  • Why they're here: He is credited with popularizing rap-singing and R&B sensibilities in hip-hop, and he founded the Toronto label OVO Sound.5
  • Start with: Take Care.

Jazz Cartier

  • From / based: Toronto.6
  • Active since: 2010.6
  • Releases to know: Marauding in Paradise (2015), Hotel Paranoia (2016), Fleurever (2018).6
  • Why they're here: Hotel Paranoia won the Juno Award for Rap Recording of the Year in 2017, and both mixtapes were longlisted for the Polaris Music Prize.6
  • Start with: Marauding in Paradise.

Sean Leon

  • From / based: Toronto.7
  • Active since: 2013.7
  • Releases to know: Ninelevenne, the Tragedy (2013), I Think You've Gone Mad (Or the Sins of the Father) (2017).7
  • Why they're here: He founded the label IXXI Initiative in 2012, which supported fellow Torontonian Daniel Caesar early on, and he co-wrote Justin Bieber's "Peaches".7
  • Start with: I Think You've Gone Mad (Or the Sins of the Father).

NAV

  • From / based: Toronto.8
  • Active since: 2015.8
  • Releases to know: Bad Habits (2019), Good Intentions (2020), OMW2 Rexdale (2025).8
  • Why they're here: He started out as a producer, with a production credit on Drake's 2015 single "Back to Back", before signing to The Weeknd's XO label in 2017.8
  • Start with: Bad Habits.

88GLAM

  • From / based: Toronto (duo of 88Camino and Derek Wise).9
  • Active since: 2017. The source lists them as active until 2022.9
  • Releases to know: 88Glam (2017), 88Glam2 (2018).9
  • Why they're here: Both members came out of the group Get Home Safe with Jazz Cartier, and the duo signed to XO and Republic in 2018.9
  • Start with: 88Glam.

Clairmont The Second

  • From / based: Toronto.10
  • Active since: 2013.10
  • Releases to know: Quest for Milk and Honey (2016), Lil Mont from the Ave (2017).10
  • Why they're here: He produces and directs as well as raps. Lil Mont from the Ave was nominated for Rap Recording of the Year at the 2018 Junos, and he won the 2019 Prism Prize Hi-Fidelity Award for music video work.10
  • Start with: Lil Mont from the Ave.

The current wave: drill, trap and darker rap (2015–now)

Pressa

  • From / based: Toronto; grew up in Driftwood, in the Jane and Finch area.11
  • Active since: 2015.11
  • Releases to know: "Novacane", "Canada Goose" (2017), Prestige (2019).11
  • Why they're here: "Novacane" was his breakout single, and he appeared in 6ix Rising, a Canadian documentary about the country's hip-hop culture.11
  • Start with: "Novacane".

KILLY

  • From / based: Toronto (he spent part of his childhood in Victoria, B.C., then moved back to Scarborough as a teenager).12
  • Active since: 2014.12
  • Releases to know: "Killamonjaro" (2017), Surrender Your Soul (2018).12
  • Why they're here: "Killamonjaro" is certified Platinum in Canada and Surrender Your Soul is certified Gold by Music Canada.12
  • Start with: "Killamonjaro".

Smiley

  • From / based: Born in Montreal; Toronto is his base. Wikipedia lists his origin as Haitian and Mexican.13
  • Active since: 2014.13
  • Releases to know: Buy or Bye (2018), "Over the Top" featuring Drake (2021), Buy or Bye 2 (2021).13
  • Why they're here: He signed with OVO Sound in 2021, and "Over the Top" reached number 13 on the Canadian Hot 100.13
  • Start with: "Over the Top".

Haviah Mighty

  • From / based: Brampton, Ontario (Greater Toronto Area).14
  • Active since: 2016, as a member of the group the Sorority.14
  • Releases to know: Flower City (2017), 13th Floor (2019), Stock Exchange (2021).14
  • Why they're here: 13th Floor won the 2019 Polaris Music Prize, and Stock Exchange won the 2022 Juno Award for Rap Album/EP of the Year.14
  • Start with: 13th Floor.

Young Hadene

  • From / based: Based in Toronto (per his official site).15
  • Active since: At least 2021, the year of the single Street Lights on Apple Music.16
  • Releases to know: Different Breed (2024), Sacrifices & Success (2025), They Took Me Down (2026).16
  • Why they're here: He makes drill and dark trap,15 and Apple Music credits his releases to ℗ str8hitsrecords.16 He's on this list because this is his site, which we've disclosed above.
  • Start with: the "Broken" video, posted to his YouTube channel on September 22, 2026.17

How the eras connect

Read the list top to bottom and a few threads run through it.

Caribbean roots run through the whole timeline. Michie Mee was born in Jamaica. Kardinal Offishall's records lean on reggae and dancehall. Smiley is of Haitian and Mexican origin. For more on that history, see our guide to Haitian rappers in Toronto and across Canada.

Toronto artists keep building their own infrastructure. Drake founded OVO Sound and Sean Leon founded IXXI Initiative. Several artists here moved between their own setups and major labels, and 88GLAM did both in the space of a few years.

Producers and directors matter as much as rappers. NAV produced before he rapped, and Clairmont The Second directs his own videos. k-os wrote and produced most of his albums himself.

The city's drill and trap sound has a history. Pressa, KILLY and Smiley came up in the mid-2010s, and newer drill artists build on that. If you're new to the genre, start with what Toronto drill music is, then see how dark trap differs from drill.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the top Toronto rappers right now?
There's no neutral way to rank them, so we don't. The current-wave section above covers Pressa, KILLY, Smiley, Haviah Mighty and Young Hadene, and the streaming-era section covers Drake, Jazz Cartier, Sean Leon, NAV, 88GLAM and Clairmont The Second.

Who is the most successful rapper from Toronto?
By chart record, Drake. His fourth album Views spent 13 weeks at number one on the Billboard 200, according to Wikipedia's summary of his career.5

Who were Toronto's first well-known rappers?
Maestro Fresh Wes and Michie Mee. Maestro's Symphony in Effect (1989) was the first platinum album by a Black Canadian artist, and Michie Mee was the first Canadian MC signed to a major American label, in 1988.12

Are there Haitian rappers from Toronto?
Yes. Smiley is Toronto-based and of Haitian and Mexican origin, according to Wikipedia.13 Our guide to Haitian rappers in Toronto and across Canada covers more artists, with sources.

Why is Toronto called the T-dot?
Kardinal Offishall's "BaKardi Slang" (2001) popularized "T-dot".3

Is this list sponsored?
No one paid to be on this list. This site is run by Young Hadene's team, and he's one of the 15 artists here.
