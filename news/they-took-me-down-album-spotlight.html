'They Took Me Down': Inside Young Hadene's 11-Track Statement

Eleven tracks. One story: the journey from Haiti to the 6ix. They Took Me Down is Young Hadene's most complete project to date — dark trap production, drill-rooted cadence, and melodic hooks carrying themes of loyalty, ambition, struggle, and personal growth.

The album consolidates the sound fans know from singles like No Sleep, 6ix Side, Pressure, and After Dark — heavy 808s, haunting melodies, and space-driven delivery — stretched across a full-length arc with room for both aggression and melody.

The sound

What separates the project from standard Toronto drill is the Haitian-Toronto fusion: Caribbean rhythmic sensibility threaded through cinematic trap atmospheres. Structured cadence shifts keep verses moving between rapid-fire delivery and sung hooks without losing the thread.

The moment

The album arrives with independent press momentum, including a feature from Exposed Vocals (see our coverage), and anchors the artist's 2026 run as one of the underground names pushing Toronto drill forward.
