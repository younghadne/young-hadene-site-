Young Hadene Launches Official Newsroom for Toronto Drill & Dark Trap Coverage

Toronto, ON — Young Hadene has opened an official newsroom at younghadene.ca/news: a dedicated home for first-hand release news, show announcements, video drops, and Toronto drill and dark trap scene coverage — separate from the artist's long-running culture blog.

Unlike the blog, which publishes evergreen guides and scene essays, the newsroom carries only timely, dated reporting: what happened, when, and where to hear or see it.

Held to newsroom standards

The newsroom launches with a public Editorial Policy (ownership, masthead, accuracy standards, AI-assistance disclosure) and a Corrections Policy with an open correction log. Every article carries a named byline, publication date, and structured NewsArticle data.

  • Tips & corrections: contact@younghadene.ca
  • Press & bookings: contact page
  • Feeds: News RSS · News sitemap

Follow the coverage: Instagram, YouTube, Spotify.

Correction notice: none to date. Spotted an error? See our Corrections Policy or email contact@younghadene.ca.
