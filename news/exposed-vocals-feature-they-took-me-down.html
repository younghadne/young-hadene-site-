Exposed Vocals Features Young Hadene's 11-Track Album 'They Took Me Down'

Independent music outlet Exposed Vocals has featured Young Hadene's new 11-track album They Took Me Down — a milestone moment for the Haitian-Toronto drill and dark trap artist and a sign that the 6ix underground is getting national press attention.

The feature covers the story behind the project: eleven tracks built on heavy 808s, cinematic dark-trap production, and the structured cadence shifts that define Young Hadene's sound — loyalty, ambition, struggle, and personal growth, carried from Haiti to Toronto.

Why this feature matters

For an independent artist with no label machine, editorial coverage is the currency that matters. Exposed Vocals spotlights emerging artists for industry and fan audiences alike, and the placement puts They Took Me Down in front of listeners who follow the underground closely.

"This one is for everyone who supported from day one," the artist said of the project in a statement published alongside the feature. The full backstory is documented in our thank-you editorial.

Where to stream

They Took Me Down is available on all major platforms — hear it via the music page, Spotify, and YouTube.
