Editorial Policy

Who we are, how we report, and the standards behind every story.

Who runs this newsroom

Young Hadene News is the official newsroom of Toronto drill and dark trap artist Young Hadene, published from Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The Editor is Young Hadene. Day-to-day publishing is handled by the artist's own team — every article carries a named byline so readers always know who wrote it.

Ownership & funding

This publication is independently owned and funded by the artist. There are no outside investors, parent companies, or advertisers influencing coverage. When we cover our own releases, shows, or collaborations, we say so in the article — sponsored or paid content, when it rarely appears, is clearly labelled and never presented as independent reporting.

What we cover

Official announcements (releases, videos, shows), first-hand studio and tour reporting, and Toronto drill / dark trap scene news where we have direct knowledge or on-the-record sources. We do not rewrite other outlets' reporting as our own — scene coverage links to its original sources.

Accuracy standards

  • Names, dates, venues, and release details are checked against primary sources before publishing.
  • Quotes are published only with the speaker's permission and are never fabricated.
  • Every article shows its publication date and, when updated, a modification date.
  • Mistakes are corrected openly under our Corrections Policy.

AI assistance disclosure

Drafting tools, including AI assistants, may help with outlines, transcripts, and copy-editing. Every article is reviewed, fact-checked, and approved by the Editor before publication, and the Editor takes full responsibility for its accuracy.

Contact the newsroom

News tips, corrections, and press inquiries: contact@younghadene.ca or via the contact page.
