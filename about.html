The Story

From Haiti to the 6ix

Young Hadene was born in Haiti, spent part of his childhood in Florida, and is now based in Toronto — a journey he describes in his Exposed Vocals interview as shaping his sound and perspective. Both places live in his music, which sits at the meeting point of drill rhythm, dark trap atmosphere, and melodic delivery — heavy 808s and cinematic production carrying lyrics about loyalty, ambition, struggle, and personal growth.

He works as an independent artist through Str8hitsRecords, releasing his own music and visuals and distributing across Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and SoundCloud. Recent releases include "No Sleep," "6ix Side," "Pressure," and "After Dark," and his eleven-track project They Took Me Down arrived October 2, 2026 — covered by Exposed Vocals. See all press, interviews, and features.

What sets the music apart is movement: structured shifts between aggressive verses and melodic hooks, rather than one gear all the way through. For the wider context behind that blend, read Haitian-Canadian music and Toronto drill and browse the discography.

Influences

Toronto Hip Hop

Drawing from the city's rich hip hop lineage.

Young Hadene draws from Toronto's rich hip hop lineage — from K-OS and Jazz Cartier to Sean Leon and 88GLAM — while incorporating Haitian musical elements and the heavy 808-driven sound of drill and trap.

Influential Toronto rappers include Drake, K-OS, Jazz Cartier, Sean Leon, 88GLAM, Night Lovell, Swollen Members, SHAD, and Clairmont The Second. Young Hadene carries this tradition forward with his own Haitian-Toronto dark trap sound.
